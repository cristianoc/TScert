TScert · method effects in practice

Using method effects: examples and outcomes

TScert checks type claims about TypeScript programs: it verifies a function when it proves the function safe, and otherwise refuses it (next slides). A function can state what it may change, and TScert checks the statement against the function’s body. Statements are certified (next slides) on test cases and on 61 functions of TScert’s own parser for TypeScript (“What expr’s statement rests on”). Of 16 parser functions measured, 11 verify (census of 2026-10-24): 9 verify in the census before the statements too, and 2 verify relative to statements that are audited, not certified, among them that of expr, the parser’s expression parser, at one call of Array map (“pattern’s map relies on the expression parser’s statement”; what it takes and what to expect follow there). The 16 are described on “More terms, for TScert’s own source”.
