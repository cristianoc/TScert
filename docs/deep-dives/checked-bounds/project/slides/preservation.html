Frame conditions

the preservation rules proved

A fact survives a step that leaves its locations unchanged

A fact over the heap reads particular locations: fields, variables, array elements and lengths. An operation’s frame condition, its frame, bounds what it may change in each class of location: values (fields and variables) and stores (an array’s elements and length), each kept (keep), changed keeping every element present (grow), or changed freely (any). The producer proposes a function’s frame from its body and the checker certifies it; a signature may also state it (slide 10).
