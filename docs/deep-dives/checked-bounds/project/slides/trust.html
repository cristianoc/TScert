Soundness and trust

What an accepted certificate guarantees

Proved in Lean: proved

Acceptance implies modeled safety for every function of the accepted unit, given its input types, entry facts, separation requirements and environment contracts; each normal return meets the checked result facts. Safety admits exceptions and divergence.

Untrusted: proposed

The producer’s types and invariants, and the certificates the search proposes from them.

Trusted computing base: trusted

  • Source and annotation translation; the model’s correspondence with JavaScript.
  • Lean’s kernel and compiler, and the executed checker.
  • Native contracts and the standard-object assumptions.
  • External callers meeting exported contracts, a stand-in until applications are analyzed too.
  • Typed arrays over fixed-size, never-detached buffers.
