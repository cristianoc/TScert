  • Candidates: the producer proposes types, and at each statement entry candidate facts: bounds, linear facts and separations, a loop head’s invariant among them.
  • Proof search: untrusted search keeps at each entry the candidates every edge into it establishes: it drops each candidate an edge does not establish and searches again, at most once per candidate, and constructs certificates.
  • Checking: the checker validates each entry’s facts on every incoming edge, and that they include the entry’s required facts: its bindings’ types and contents, and a written @invariant.
  • Arithmetic: certificates use nonnegative linear combinations, integer rounding and disequalities; search uses Fourier–Motzkin elimination.
  • Diagnostics: a refused unit lists where the producer’s proposal drops a fact and what dropped it (a statement’s change, a call’s frame, slide 8), and each candidate the search left out, with the edge that does not establish it.
