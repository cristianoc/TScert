Contract support

C is a clause of the shared language

Preconditions, postconditions and invariants

ContractSupport
Preconditionwritten as @requires C; checked at analyzed calls, assumed by the body
Postconditionwritten as @ensures C; checked at every normal return, held by the variable a call’s result is assigned to
Type invariantwritten as @invariant C on a type; obligations at function boundaries
Loop invariantwritten as @invariant C before a loop, required on every edge into the head; or inferred, a candidate kept where every such edge establishes it

Clauses are linear relations over values, field paths and lengths, under null guards; quantified clauses forall i in xs: B over an array’s elements; and a !== b of two arrays (slide 10). A heap change preserves no quantified fact: a stated limit.
