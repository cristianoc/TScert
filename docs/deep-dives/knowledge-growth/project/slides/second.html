  • How it was found: the six costly functions of “Where it stands” were measured one by one against main, after their total had fallen; the total hid it.
  • What was observed: separation stored as single pairs, one per field on each side, sorted at every point, and restated on every slot of both classes whenever a step or an edge read it. Removing that removed the rise.
  • Fix: separation stored grouped (SeparationNormal.compact, proved to state exactly what the separation states). The checks that read it, at an edge and at a write, compare by class instead of restating (checkBy, apartBy, proved sound).
