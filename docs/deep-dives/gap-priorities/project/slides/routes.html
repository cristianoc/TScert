Choose the first route that establishes the obligation at acceptable cost

RouteRequired evidence
SupportA checked rule or audited external contract admits the construct
Runtime checkAn executable check establishes the complete required property
RepairThe source corrects a defect exposed by the refusal
AvoidanceAn equivalent source form preserves returns, exceptions and effects
Host boundaryA small wrapper performs an external service operation; callers assume its audited contract
Trusted unitThe owner accepts a local unit’s complete contract as an assumption
ProofA Lean proof establishes the required fact in the model
  • First inspect whether the source discards information its own construction already establishes. A negative case needs demonstrated unsafe behavior. A refused ordinary program remains an obligation to investigate, even when another source form verifies. The status table in WORK.md owns each investigation’s status.
