  • A case example: is the smallest source with the aspect's refusal, and its header lists the census functions it stands for. The test suite (make suite) checks its expected outcome on every run.
  • A gap case: is a safe program TScert refuses for want of a rule or a contract. A negative case is a refusal that is right: the program could produce a wrong value.
  • Which aspects get one: every aspect blocking more than 1,000 statements or more than 40 functions: nine aspects. Seven of them had gap cases in the suite, nine cases among them; the other two get one first (next slide).
  • Closing a case: a fix makes it verify, and the census copies of its functions then pass that refusal and stop at the next.
