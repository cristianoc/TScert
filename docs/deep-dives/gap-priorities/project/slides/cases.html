  • A case example: is the smallest source with the cause's refusal; one made from the census names in its header the census functions it stands for. The test suite (make suite) checks its expected outcome on every run.
  • A gap case: is a safe program TScert refuses for want of a rule or a contract. A negative case is a refusal that is right: the program could produce a wrong value.
  • Which causes get one: every cause meeting at least 10 functions, where its smallest source is found; three have none yet (marked no case). Earlier censuses made most of the cases the next slides name; five are made from this census: blocker-type-alias-guard-member, blocker-indexed-optional-method, blocker-optional-method-bind, blocker-node-url-import and blocker-array-generic-annotation.
  • Closing a case: a fix makes it verify, and the census copies of its functions then pass that refusal and stop at the next.
