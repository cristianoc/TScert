Decompose an actual execution inside a call of C into a run of outside code

no-behaviour-change

Why

The read-only rule's lemmas took a run of outside code as given: each outside step's roots, reach, writes, no-forge and kept marks were hypotheses, and the history was any sequence of transitions, a hold among them. An actual execution is a sequence of steps of the calculus; its call structure, its roots and its history are read from its terms, so those hypotheses are discharged and what remains is stated as the interface the checks and the certified effects meet.

addedDefinition read-only handles: configurations and their states

a configuration is an indexed heap and a term valid at its world; an
actual step is a Step between their heaps with an admissible world
transition; the state of a configuration with a ghost has the roots
vals(ofC C, not ofC C c, t) of its term

addedTheorem an actual step of outside code is an outside step

AStep x ythe active frame of x runs no code of CRow(Θ)
⟹OutsideStep(Θ) from x's state to y's, the ghost the one its
allocation extends (old sites and keys kept, new locations outside
storage, E, A and B unchanged)

addedDefinition read-only handles: the call structure

depthC(t) = the number of blocks of frames running C's code on t's
path; a step is outside, an entry, a step inside a call or an exit by
its active frame and the change of depthC; the call discipline Disc:
a step of outside code raises depthC by at most one, a step of C's
code lowers it by at most one, and neither moves it the other way

addedDefinition read-only handles: the history of an execution

an entry pushes the protected set, then its incoming values cross; a

closures' bindings, or storage of its sites); no hold transition

with the sites, keys and crossings the checks give and the fields of

exit), and each checked step keeping each invariant of GC; Cert.member

Run(Θ, Ψ, G, n, d) between the first and last states, n its length;

Prove the call discipline of well-formed terms and the world of a safe step

no-behaviour-change

Why

The decomposition of an actual execution took the call discipline and each step's world transition as hypotheses. The discipline is a property of the terms an execution reaches: runs sit at evaluation positions, so a step enters at most one. The world transition is the one a safe term's step already carries.

addedDefinition read-only handles: terms free of runs

free(false, t) ⟺ t holds no goto and no frame, the code of its values
included; free(true, t) ⟺ t holds no frame and every goto in it has
code, environment and default free of runs (a pending run, as a call
of graph code with a rest leaves under the rest's binding)

addedDefinition read-only handles: well-formed runtime terms

wf(t) ⟺ along t's path every other subterm of a position is pending,
a goto or frame on it has its parts free of runs, a frame's running
term is well formed; HeapFree(h) ⟺ every value of h is free of runs

addedTheorem every step keeps well-formedness

Step h t h' t'wf(t)HeapFree(h)
⟹wf(t')HeapFree(h')and the path changes as PathStep with at
most one frame pushed (PathStep1)

addedTheorem the call discipline

Step x ywf(x.t)HeapFree(x.h)⟹Disc(F, C, c, x, y) at every
component; so XStep needs no discipline premise from a well-formed
configuration (XStep.ofwf)

addedCounterexample

letE null (frame_C (frame_out (goto_C))) steps by letV from no call ofC active to two: Disc fails for arbitrary terms
