Restore the model rules of the note "Tolerant frames" and check their axioms

Why

The pruning of declarations the single pass does not reach removed the theorems that state the note's atoms' axioms, its rules and its worked loop in the indexed model: no root named them, and the note is no document under docs/. Module headers, the note's errata and TolerantFramesElems still cited them. They are restored with their statements and proofs unchanged, and each is a root of the axiom check, so a later pruning by reachability keeps them.
