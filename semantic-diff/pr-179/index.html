Compose the summaries of a sequence on both exits, rebasing the later frame with absorption

no-behaviour-change

Why

The note's Seq-Eff composes one effect, the normal one, and leaves its rebasing premise informal; Post.compose_at uses RebaseOk, class evidence valid at the entry and a checked inclusion, and its rebasing also lets an earlier any entry absorb a location the later frame covers (design corrections 3 and 4). A command's summary also has an exceptional exit, and an uncaught throw of the second command includes the first command's effect. Composing postconditions must keep the intermediate state: reading the first's post at the second's two-state identifies their different old states.

no-behaviour-change

Why

A branch has two alternative executions; its summary must bound both at each exit. The design joins alternatives by an upper bound and keeps only postconditions both establish, derived by checked consequence where they differ syntactically. The sequence's exceptional exit is such a join too: the first command's throw, or its return followed by the second's throw.

Why

The note's Loop rule takes {I ∧ □Φ R} c {I}, c : Φ and Φ rigid, and concludes {I ∧ □Φ R} loop c {I ∧ □Φ R}. Over summaries a loop's effect is bounded relative to the loop's entry, while each iteration's summary is read where that iteration starts, so the bound must be closed under one more traversal after rebasing; and the frame □Φ R needs rigidity of Φ as a whole, which the parity evidence states on a domain. A bound on the iteration's exit alone gives no bound inside it.

State a signature's contract from its body's summary at the call's positions

no-behaviour-change

Why

The design takes a function's inferred effect to be its body's summary: the body frame at the call positions, the exported postconditions checked consequences in the interface scope. A body's summary must then give the contract a signature states, at the two-state its summary is read at, on both exits.

addedDefinition a body's runs at a call's positions, and a signature's scope

AtCall(R, r, args) c v m s s' ⟺ R c (callEnv r args ⇒ ensuresEnv v r args, m, s ⇒ s')
Scopedn(S) ⟺ frames read call positions ≤ n, posts entry slots ≤ n, exit slots ≤ n + 1

addedTheorem from a body's summary to its signature

S sound for the body from P, S.c ⊑ Sig.c at each exit, entry meets P
⟹ Sig.c holds at callState for every run at the call's positions
Sig = summary(e) at arity |args|, actuals values ⟹
return v: postW(e); throw: excPost(e)

Gap

The body's runs are presented at the call's valuations. A body whose exit valuation holds locals beside the call's slots needs a scope transfer: a formula scoped at (n + 1, n + 2) holds alike at two-states whose valuations agree below the scope (frames by EffectAction.congr_roots, assertions by sat_scoped_congr; linear facts, transports and operational posts still to prove).
