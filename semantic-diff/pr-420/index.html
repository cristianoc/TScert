Why

buildAndPatchPreservedSpace maintains parallel arrays flags (Uint8Array), advances, and extras where PRESERVED_SPACE (tag 2 under mask 0x7) entries never carry extras: forall i in extras: (flags[i] & 0x7) !== 2 || extras[i] === null After the loop, when (flags[patchAt]! & KIND_BITS) === PRESERVED_SPACE, it overwrites advances[patchAt] = [5, 5, 5]. The work is pull request #420, rebased onto the interval quantified clauses of #419: each rule below is stated over an interval [a, b).
