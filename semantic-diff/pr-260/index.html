Why

Write one length term for arrays, typed arrays and strings in both papers

no-behaviour-change

Why

The owner approved writing one term |pi|, JavaScript's length of what pi reads, where the papers wrote three: |pi| for an array's spine, view(pi) for a typed array and strlen(pi) for a string.
