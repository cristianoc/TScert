2da714b29 · Cristiano Calcagno · Nov 16, 09:19

Proposed: name the spine, define indexed collections, and kind the regions at a path

no-behaviour-change

Why

The owner found "store" a misleading name: in the literature a store is the whole heap, and in a Modifies annotation the word misreads. He chose "spine", in the semantics and the annotations alike. He then organised the values as objects, of which the indexed collections are those with a spine, and asked that a region such as the spine of a path be written only where the path has the kind the region needs, since a region at a value of another kind has no meaning. The session that owns the technical content accepted the three changes, gave the statements of the kinding and checked the resulting text.
