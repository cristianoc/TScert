Compose a rest body's exits with its rest allocation at the exits

no-behaviour-change

Why

A function with a rest parameter runs its body after allocating the rest array, so its body's exits are stated from the heap that allocation leaves, while the call's guarantee is stated from the call's entry. The proof converted the body's exits to heap postconditions, carried those across the allocation one field at a time (Effect.post_prefix, excPost_prefix) and converted back: a round trip through the old fields that the change of the effect type removes. The general rule, an exit from a later entry is an exit from an earlier one by Seq-Eff for the frame and consequence for the postconditions, had no statement at the level of exits, and the rebasing it needs sat above body typing.
