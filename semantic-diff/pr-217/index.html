State support once, over any domain of states, for both assertion languages

no-behaviour-change

Why

The paper's Definition "Support" was stated twice: once over arbitrary heaps for the acceptance path's facts, once over indexed states for the paper layer's assertions, each with its own body and, on the acceptance side, its own composition lemmas. The migration to one support relation, one respect check and one frame theorem (docs/drafts/rules-as-mechanism.md) starts by making the relation one.

addedDefinition support over a domain of states

A domain D gives each state s a heap D.heap(s) and admits transitions
D.adm(s, s').
SupportsD(C, A, S) ⟺ ∀ ρ m s s'. D.adm(s, s') ∧ C(ρ, m, s, s') ∧
ρ, m, s ⊨ A ∧ Supported(S, ρ, D.heap(s), D.heap(s')) ⟹ ρ, m, s' ⊨ A
where Supported(S, ρ, h, h') says every location of each region r of
an entry r : δ of S, read at h, changes from h to h' within δ.

modifiedDefinition support on the acceptance path

modifiedDefinition support in the paper layer

addedTheorem a support over all heaps holds in every domain

Supportsheaps(C, A, S) ⟹
SupportsD(C at the heaps of D, A at the heap of D, S)

modifiedTheorem weakening, invariance, conjunction, disjunction, a larger support

Check respect once, under a query, for both assertion languages

no-behaviour-change

Why

The paper's Definition "Respect" was checked by three loops: respects under a separation query on the acceptance path, a copy of it under separation knowledge (stable), and the paper layer's certificate check, which walked support and effect entries by index and had its own pairwise soundness proof. One respect check and one soundness theorem are what the migration to one frame rule consumes.

modifiedCheck the paper layer's respect certificates

modifiedDefinition the stability check under separation knowledge

removedTheorem a transition within an effect is within a support whose every pair is ordered or disjoint

every e ∈ S, e' ∈ Φ ordered or disjoint ⟹ Supported(S) along an action of Φ
(the paper layer's certificates use respectsound instead)

modifiedTheorem soundness of the paper's respect certificates

Soundness

A certificate is checked against the pair of regions it answers for; the query's soundness is each certificate's soundness, so dropping the index positions accepts only pairs some certificate proves disjoint.
