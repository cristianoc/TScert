Derive goals from their restatement through required field reads

verified

Why

Returning { chunks } where chunks satisfies a quantified fact did not establish the same fact about result.chunks: the knowledge links result.chunks to chunks, but the goal check compared the goal's array root result.chunks with the known fact's root chunks and refused. The same held at a call passing { chunks } where a type's quantified invariant is required, and for literals nested in literals. Combines #401 (the restatement lowerRequiredEvery and its proof) and #411's first commit (the general goal check as its site, chains of reads).
