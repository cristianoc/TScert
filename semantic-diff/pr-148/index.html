Why

On measureAnalysis the single pass spent about a quarter of its samples there, the search and check much more in eraseDups.

Linear facts added (Linear.Known)

a knowledge's facts prepared once: each fact once by hashing, held facts before extra ones, a table from normal form to the ≤ fact of least bound, and a table from variable to facts. Known.direct answers a goal one held fact gives (same normal form, bound at most the goal's) by one lookup; otherwise the search runs over the facts connected to the goal, found through the variable table (Known.near), with the strict bounds of the disequalities among them, and with the extra facts only where they add connected facts.
