Rebase step regions to their class, and fresh stores at any path depth

no-behaviour-change

Why

Composing a step's effect into the invocation's rebased every region it could not link to the entry to all, so a step writing an element cell or a field reached through an unlinked path was bounded as if it could change every location, a stronger obligation than the note's rebasing rule, which widens such a region to its class. Freshness of a selected store was recognized only at a position or one field below it. The review of the class-region commits also found comments and draft wording still describing role-based selection.
