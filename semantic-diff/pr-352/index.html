Preserve checked field-read links at loop heads

verified

Why

A local copy of an array field lost its connection to the field at a loop head, so a bound established through the handle could not justify indexing through the copy.

modifiedProposal head candidates

Propose existing heap read links for immutable copies, including object destructuring. Check each proposal on every incoming edge and drop links a predecessor cannot establish.

modifiedRule call frame

Retain checked read links on normal call return when the call's
footprint preserves their supporting field.

modifiedTheorem single-pass soundness

Carry the additional checked links through head entry and prove their
preservation using the existing footprint and heap-link lemmas.

addedExample

Accept three safe variants and reject both ways of replacing the fieldon the back edge. Both negative controls yield NaN under Node.
