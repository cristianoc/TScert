Hold a local written inside an expression in a slot, rebound where the write runs

verified
pipeline/examples/stated-slot-collect.ts#collectpipeline/examples/stated-slot-conditional.ts#merged

Why

A body stating a frame refused growing an array it allocates lazily across a call. collect (step(runs, s); (acc ??= []).push(s); in a loop, step and collect stating Modifies<{ values: "any"; "runs.open[*]": "any" }, …>) was refused at the push: "the frame stated for 'collect' is not established by its body". acc, written inside its statement, was a cell, and a cell's "contents are fresh" fact is a fact of the heap, which step's frame (values: "any") may change: the frame admits replacing the cell's contents with an old array. The binding had to be a cell only because the lowering typed the rest of a statement, and joined an expression's arms, at the frame the statement entered with, so a slot rebound inside the statement did not fit (the nestedWrite conjunct of "Slot or cell", stated there as a lowering limit). A slot's value is part of the environment, and "null or an array this run allocated" is a fact of the value alone, which no heap step changes.

modifiedDefinition lowering: slot or cell

modifiedLowering expressions: the environment each part leaves

modifiedDefinition producer: facts and handler states after a nested write

Soundness
