Produce an index fact's support with finite evidence, checked against the shared support relation

no-behaviour-change

Why

The acceptance path's support of an index fact was one function, IndexFact.support?, with one soundness theorem over its result. The migration to one support relation (docs/drafts/rules-as-mechanism.md) reads each assertion language's supports as a check of finite evidence that concludes the shared Supports, so that both languages' supports enter the frame theorem the same way.

addedDefinition an assertion language's supports, by evidence, over a domain

SupportLanguage(D) = formulas F with a meaning sat(F), contexts c with a
condition C(c), evidence ev, and check(c, F, S, ev) : Bool with
check(c, F, S, ev) ⟹ SupportsD(C(c), sat(F), S)

addedDefinition the evidence of an index fact's support and its check

ev ::= slots | length | bound | linear | either(SA, evA, SB, evB)
| guarded(SB, evB) | spineApart | apart | every
check(c, F, S, ev): S is the support the form of ev gives F; for
F ∨ G, S = SA ++ SB with each part checked; for a guarded fact,
S = reads(guard) ++ SB with the body checked; for every(x.π, t, φ),
φ reads no element's fields; an element fact has no evidence

modifiedDefinition the support of an index fact

addedTheorem a checked support supports its fact; a produced support checks

check(c, F, S, ev) ⟹ Supports(c.Holds, F, S)
produce(c, F) = (S, ev) ⟹ check(c, F, S, ev)

modifiedTheorem every produced support supports its fact
