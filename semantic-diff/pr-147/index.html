Check a unit's searched evidence once in its search, not twice

no-behaviour-change

Why

A unit's search (verifier --produce-unit) checked its graph twice. The search of each graph ends by checking the certificate it built against the unit's claims and program, and its refusal there is the search's located diagnostic. The group's search then encoded its evidence, decoded it again and ran the whole request check over it, graph, codes and claims included, before the check stage checked it a third time. On pretext's buildEngineProfile the two checks inside the search were 2/3 of its 42.5 s; on measureAnalysis and prepareRichInline a quarter.

addedDefinition search: what a searched request leaves to check

A response the search of a request returned has passed the request's
validity, its code table (its codes' entries in order) and its graph's
acceptance under the search's own check. What remains of the request
check is its assertions, its effect, its postcondition, its store
regions and its obligations. The group's search checks those over the
bundle it built, before encoding, and reports the checker's located
refusal of them as the search's, as before.

Compare an edge's implications by slot in the checker and drop repeats by hash in the search

no-behaviour-change

Why

An edge carries the implications its source node states (test = true ⟹ target : T), and every implication its target node states must follow from one of them. The check compared each target implication with every source one, after removing duplicates from the source list pairwise: quadratic in the implications per node. In buildEngineProfile 255,709 implications are carried at 760 of its 932 nodes, up to 1,242 at one, and this comparison was most of its search and of its check.

addedDefinition checker: implications by slot

index(xs)(a, b) = [ y.type | y ∈ xs, y.test = a, y.target = b ]
follows(x) ⟺ ∃ T ∈ index(xs)(m(x.test), m(x.target)). T ⊆ x.type
∨ Γ[m(x.target)] ⊆ x.type ∨ Γ[m(x.test)] never holds true
where m is the edge's slot mapping and xs the source's implications at
every slot its alias links relate, duplicates kept.

addedTheorem the compiled check equals the proved one

impliesCheck Γ (aliasImplications links xs) m targets
= impliesCheckIndexed Γ links xs m targets
The verifier runs carriesIndexed in place of carries (a @[csimp]
equation, which the axiom check covers); the proofs keep reasoning
about carries.
