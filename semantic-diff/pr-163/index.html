Draft the theory of fact preservation as tolerant frames

no-behaviour-change

Why

Two mechanisms decide that a heap fact survives a step: the footprint and support judgment with its frame theorem, used for the facts map relies on, and the term-by-term preservation of index facts. No document states what they have in common, which patterns of heap reasoning the code needs, or how the result relates to separation logic. The owner asked for a conceptual draft answering those, written with review by Astra.

addedDocs drafts/tolerant-frames.md

Fifteen patterns from pretext and the cases; facts as depending on observations with tolerances; the two sources of a bound, the frame and an invariant, and exact posts; the tolerant frame theorem (stable_sound) with region logic's frame rule and stability under interference as its two extremes; composition by rebasing, and freshness as a bound on effects only; the forms of separation in use and their depth; each pattern's derivation; the position among dynamic frames, rely/guarantee, monotone state and separation logic; soundness of a node's checked knowledge transformer as the central statement and the forward pass's obligations; limits; which statements are proved and which three are conjectured.

modifiedDocs REFERENCES.md

Entries for Kassios 2006, Banerjee, Naumann and Rosenberg 2013, Jones 1983, Ahmed, Dreyer and Rossberg 2009, Dreyer, Neis and Birkedal 2010, Calcagno, O'Hearn and Yang 2007, Dinsdale-Young et al. 2013 and Birkedal, Torp-Smith and Yang 2005, each checked against its publication record.
