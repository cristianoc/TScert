Refuse the central example's aliased call, and !== of typed arrays

refused

Why

measure's precondition flags !== runs.openBrackets is what lets it keep i < flags.length across readRuns(runs); a caller passing one array as both sides breaks it: under Node, aliased([1, 2, 3]) splices the array it sums and returns NaN. Two typed arrays are distinct objects that may view one buffer, so !== of views states no separation of their storage.

the call measure(xs, { openBrackets: xs }) has no store separation of xs from the literal's field: the field's read link reaches xs itself, which no region fact or separation sets apart

addedRefusal the clause atom !== of typed arrays

/** @requires a !== b */ over two Uint8Array parameters is refused at the clause, with its location
