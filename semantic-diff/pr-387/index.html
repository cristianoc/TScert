Why

Five #356 paperAssume sites remained in Pretext's layout.ts (lines 168, 309, 357) and rich-inline.ts (lines 1249, 1388): three following calls to countPreparedLines / stepPreparedLineGeometryFromStart in line-break.ts that lacked stated Effects<..., ...> signatures, and two inside inline visitor closures (layoutWithLines and walkRichInlineLineRanges) passed to walkPreparedLinesRaw where the callback captured a handle (prepared / flow) whose type invariant is heap-dependent. Unconditionally assuming a captured binding's @invariant on closure entry is unsound (broken if the caller, callee, or callback mutates the handle or if the callee escapes the closure).

addedRule paper effect language: scoped callback invariant Callback-Inv

Adds CallbackInvRule to Verification.Paper.EffectLanguage.Rules and
proves callbackInv_rule in Verification.Paper.EffectLanguage.Model
(extending model_rules to all six paper rules): if predicate P holds at
the call state s₀, is preserved by a self-stabilizing support effect Ψ,
and both Φ_own and Φ_cb are anchored and within Ψ at s₀, then P
holds at every state along any interleaved Φ_own/Φ_cb run. When the
callback does not escape the call's dynamic extent, P holds on every
callback entry.
