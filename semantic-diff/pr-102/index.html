Add cases for the assertions that refuse ten line walkers of pretext's line-break.ts

no-behaviour-change

Why

Past the literal defaults (#98), the census of pretext's src/ refuses 10 of the 16 functions of line-break.ts at a cast claim: a non-null assertion or an as of an element read, and one non-null assertion of a nullable local. Each claim was read against what holds at the function's boundary: 8 functions read an element at an index bounded by another array's length (segmentFlags[i]! inside widths.length), by a count parameter, or by no test; one asserts such a read with as; one asserts present a value another argument is taken to imply (startAdvances! where the cursor is inside a segment). None of these claims holds for every argument the signature admits, so each refusal is right and the census had no case for it.

A gap case for the one safe claim found past them: a field'stest repeated from the `if` before it, in stepPreparedSimpleLineGeometry.
