Why

Seven paperAssume sites tagged #356 remained in dev/paper-rules/pretext/src/layout.ts (1) and dev/paper-rules/pretext/src/rich-inline.ts (6), assuming segmentFlags.length === widths.length after measureAnalysis, flags.subarray(0, segmentCount), and loop-allocated zeros(count) arrays. Proving those invariants across prepare.ts, measurement.ts, han-kerning.ts, layout.ts, and rich-inline.ts required: 1. In-bounds writes to dense (Dense<T>) arrays keeping all array spines unchanged (arrays: keep). 2. Loop-head freshness (givenFresh) and Fresh-returning calls establishing spine separation (spineApart) from existing arrays. 3. views class key in Modifies<...> and a sound bounded length contract for %ByteSubarray% (TypedArray.prototype.subarray). 4. Guarded entailment combining all facts under the same guard (sameGuardBodies), complementary-guard elimination (complementaryGuardElim), complementary null/undefined guard tags at merges, read-link lowering of spineApart, and empty-path guarded spine slots in freshSpineApart.

addedRule single pass: dense in-bounds writes keep spines

ExpressionEffects.denseWriteWithin and elem_var_write_dense_core
prove that an indexed write a[i] = v to a Dense<T> array with
0 <= i < a.length leaves every array spine length unchanged.

strict .bound atoms from idx >= arr.length / idx < arr.length
guards even when idx carries .index.

addedRule single pass: loop-head freshness and fresh-call spine separation

SinglePass.headExit and HeadEstablishes.fresh verify proposed loop-head
fresh slots (Json.proposeFreshHeads). PromiseCalls.freshCallSpineApart
establishes spineApart between a Fresh-returning call's result and
existing array bindings and field paths. IndexWrites.guardedSpineSlots
includes empty-path slots named in guarded spineApart facts when
allocating fresh arrays. SurvivalSeparation.IndexFact.keepsUnder and
IndexWrites.guardedWriteCarried preserve guarded linear and separation
facts across guarded writes.

addedRule single pass: same-guard batching, complementary-guard elimination, and spineApart lowering

- HeapKnowledge.sameGuardBodies collects all unconditional guarded
atoms sharing a (lg s p, t) guard in guardEntails so multiple
facts under the same guard combine to entail a joint body.
- HeapKnowledge.compGuardPairs and complementaryGuardElim discharge
a goal entailed by both the t and t.complement body sets of a
guard pair (precomputed and deduplicated in GuardPrep).
- HeapKnowledge.lowerSpineApartFact lowers .spineApart (including
inside .guarded) across read links in lowerAtoms.
- SinglePass.mergeState and TolerantFramesMergeMirror.mergeGuarded
propose complementary .notNull/.notUndef tags when a merge splits
on null/undefined.
