Why

Step 2 of the design effects-of-commands.md moves the deciders that read an effect's flags onto the entries of its exit frames. Every such decider needs a check over a frame's region-and-change entries: which entries freshness discharges below an invocation's mark, whether the rest is within a bound, and whether the frame changes nothing. Record each visit's local certificate per exit, its grades the projection

no-behaviour-change

Why

A graded visit recorded closed grades only: the closed footprints its rules grant, already projected to the three content categories, with no exit specification behind them. Step 2 of effects-of-commands.md asks each checked visit to record, per exit, its frame and its postconditions independent of the enclosing bound, so that the later composition of command summaries reads them, with the recorded grades as their projection.

addedDefinition a step's local certificate

cert(step) = (normal: [q₁ … qₙ], exceptional: [q'₁ … q'ₘ], P, scope)
each q an exit specification (frame, postconditions), read from the
step's evidence and none from a bound; P the fresh call positions
inspection or empty type:[([], [])]
typed or initializing write: [(c.fp, []) | c a closed footprint its rule grants]
selected call, its derivation checked: normal(summary e); exceptional(summary e) where its throw is e's (onThrow)

addedDefinition the grades a certificate projects to

grades(cert) = (normal.map (⌊·.frame⌋P), exceptional.map (⌊·.frame⌋P), scope)

addedTheorem the grades are the certificate's projection

grades(cert(step)) = stepGrades(step)
record.grades = grades(record.certificate) = stepGrades(step)

modifiedDocs records of the pass

proposals-by-the-pass.md states the certificate in each record.

Effect

  • region, receiver, modifies, fresh, typed-array, bounds-pattern cases unchanged (30, 27, 10, 51, 33, 50 of each as expected)

Gap

A write's or an inspection's certificate states its frame and no postcondition; its exact post is the checked rule's output knowledge, not yet a list of formulas. Soundness of a certificate's specifications for the visit's execution is not proved here: acceptance still reads the grades, whose soundness is unchanged.
