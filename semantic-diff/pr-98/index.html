Read String.raw as a template tag: the template of its raw strings, through the overlay

verified

Why

pretext (chenglou/pretext at 0d102a5c) builds its regular expressions in the initializations of src/prepare.ts and src/line-breaks.ts as lazyRegExp(String.raw\^\p{M}\, 'u'). The global reader refused it, "the method String.raw is only called, on its host": no overlay entry read a member as a tag. The initialization statements were left out, so CLOSING_BRACKET, CURSIVE_LETTER, TAKES_MARK_SCRIPTS, extendedPictographicRe, astralProperties and webkitLineRules were unbound in the functions reading them, 7 pretext functions.

addedTrust library globals: String.raw as a template tag

raw(template: { raw: readonly string[] | ArrayLike<string>; }, ...substitutions: any[]): string; read only as a tag, String.raw…, never called or read otherwise; it joins no value. The host's steps (ECMA-262, tagged templates and String.raw): Get(String, "raw") finds the standard function under the approved policy that standard objects are not tampered with; the engine makes the frozen template object, its raw the frozen array of the raw strings, cached per site and held by no other code; the substitutions run in order; then String.raw reads raw's length and each element, strings, and appends between two of them the ToString of a substitution's value.

addedLowering the parser: String.raw as a tag

String.raw`h₀${e₁}h₁…${eₙ}hₙ`
⇝(…((h₀' + e₁) + h₁') … + eₙ) + hₙ'
hᵢ' the raw string: the source text between the delimiters, each CR LF and CR a LF(ECMA-262's TRV)

(a template without substitutions is the string h₀'; any other use of String.raw is refused)
